# Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second

One sentence, 195 characters: a startup pitch that sounds like a joke until the business logic lands.

> We rent strangers' dogs to nervous first-daters for $25 an hour, because nothing makes a stranger look kind, safe, and worth a second date like a borrowed golden retriever that already likes you.

The absurd premise — renting a stranger's dog for a date — turns on a second look: the paying customer is the nervous first-dater, the product is borrowed trust (a dog signals kindness and safety instantly), and the price is concrete at $25 an hour. The sentence names the odd thing sold, who pays, and why it holds together.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network, deterministic) writes that single sentence to `/output/entry.txt`, checks it is 1–200 characters, exactly one sentence ending in a period, a single line with no leading or trailing whitespace, and reads the file back to confirm the write is byte-identical.
